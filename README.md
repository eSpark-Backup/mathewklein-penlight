# mathewklein-penlight
It is a medical device for penlight applications. The product will consist of two PCBs namely:
1. Main PCB
2. Side PCB

## Main PCB Requirements
* Whole circuit should consist of discrete parts.
* Primary PCB shall be 10.5mm OD with a male clocking feature.
* Primary PCB shall be 2 layers.
* On the back of the Primary PCB shall be a pad that easily interfaces with the + side of a [battery](https://www.amazon.com/Rechargeable-Lithium-Battery-icr14250-300mAh/dp/B074L6Z5BC/ref=sr_1_14_sspa?hvadid=616991202707&hvdev=c&hvlocphy=9032475&hvnetw=g&hvqmt=e&hvrand=1770071664611046335&hvtargid=kwd-18872225111&hydadcr=24660_13611807&keywords=ls14250+battery&qid=1683565773&sr=8-14-spons&psc=1&spLa=ZW5jcnlwdGVkUXVhbGlmaWVyPUEyT1hRS1pOTjdQQjVFJmVuY3J5cHRlZElkPUEwMTQ1MTU0SkVFUzdLOUo2SFJUJmVuY3J5cHRlZEFkSWQ9QTA4NzM4ODkxSTlWVlpPVzZXMU0wJndpZGdldE5hbWU9c3BfbXRmJmFjdGlvbj1jbGlja1JlZGlyZWN0JmRvTm90TG9nQ2xpY2s9dHJ1ZQ==).
* The cathode of the LED shall be expose and wrapped on the wide of the primary PCB to allow for current to travel up the housing.
* The circuit shall contain resistors and other necessary components to efficiently run an A23 battery (12V) for a prolonged period of time.
* The LED shall be an SMD type White LED. Lumen intensity TBD.
* The motor shall have a frequency close to 180Hz, with vibration magnitude TBD.

## Side PCB Requirements
1. The Side PCB will have 2 x tact / dome switches. We can suggest the part to client. The forward most dome switch shall activate the LED, the other one shall active the motor.
2. Secondary PCB shall be rectangular (4x10mm).
3. The Side PCB will link with Main PCB via Flex cable.
